OmniForge stores app data in your platform user-data directory. This includes the local database, downloaded models, transcription assets, and logs.

## What is stored locally

Local data includes:

- Workspace database files.
- Indexed document metadata and vectors.
- Downloaded local models.
- Transcription model files.
- Logs and diagnostic files.

## Back up carefully

If you back up your computer, your OmniForge data may be included depending on your backup settings. Treat backups with the same care as the original files.
