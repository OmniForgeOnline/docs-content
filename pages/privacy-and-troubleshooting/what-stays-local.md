OmniForge is designed for private local document and recording workflows. Files and transcripts you add to the desktop app are processed for your local workspace.

## Local workspace content

Your workspace can include:

- Documents you add.
- Audio recordings.
- Transcripts.
- Assistant conversation history.

This content is intended to remain part of your local OmniForge environment.

## Online services

Some actions still require the internet, such as sign-in, billing, downloads and updates.

## Practical privacy habit

Use separate workspaces for sensitive projects and avoid adding files that do not belong in that workspace.
