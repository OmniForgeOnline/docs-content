OmniForge includes a Free plan and paid options for heavier use.

![OmniForge plan selection view showing Free, Starter, and one-time plan options.](/images/screenshots/pricing-plans.webp)

## Free

Free is for trying OmniForge with real work. It includes limited indexed files, one workspace, and limited recording duration.

Use Free when you are evaluating whether local document intelligence fits your workflow.

## Starter

Starter is for daily use. It removes the main limits for files, workspaces, and recording time.

Choose Starter when OmniForge becomes part of your regular work.

## One-time

The one-time option is for users who prefer a single desktop license payment instead of a recurring subscription.

Open **Plans & pricing** in the app to see current availability and checkout options.
