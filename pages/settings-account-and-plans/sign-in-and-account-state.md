An OmniForge account is required for every plan, including Free. Sign-in identifies your account and plan; it does not upload your documents, recordings or Assistant conversations for AI processing.

## Sign in

1. Open OmniForge and use the sign-in screen, or open the account menu if a session is already active.
2. Choose **Sign in**.
3. Complete the browser sign-in flow.
4. Return to OmniForge.

After sign-in, the app shows your account state and available plan actions.

## If sign-in fails

Try these steps:

1. Confirm your internet connection.
2. Close the browser tab and try again.
3. Restart OmniForge.
4. Check whether your default browser blocks the sign-in redirect.

Your local workspace data is not removed when sign-in fails.
