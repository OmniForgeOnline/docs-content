Every plan requires an OmniForge account. Plans change quotas and administrative features; document and recording processing remains local.

## Free

Free includes 50 indexed files, one workspace and 40 minutes per recording. Use it to try your documents and a short meeting before choosing a paid plan.

## Unlimited

Unlimited costs €9.99 per month or €99.99 per year. It includes unlimited files, workspaces and recording time, custom summary templates and Discord support.

## Business

Business costs €19.99 per seat per month. It includes Unlimited features and adds an admin portal, centralized billing, user management, SSO, priority support and early access.

## Business Plus

Business Plus starts with a workflow assessment. Installation, configuration, onboarding and customization are scoped after discovery. Pricing is tailored after that assessment.

See the [pricing page](https://omniforge.online/pricing) for current packaging and request a workflow assessment there if your team needs setup support.
