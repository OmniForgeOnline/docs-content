Application Settings controls how OmniForge looks and how it interprets language and dates.

## Open settings

1. Select the settings button in the top navigation.
2. Review the available sections.
3. Change the settings you need.
4. Apply the changes.

![OmniForge application settings dialog with appearance, language, timezone, updates, and support sections.](/images/screenshots/settings-dialog.webp)

## Appearance

Use appearance to switch between light and dark mode. Choose the mode that is easiest to read in your working environment.

## Language

Language is used for default AI translation behavior. It does not translate every part of the app interface.

## Timezone and date format

Timezone and date format help OmniForge display dates consistently and reason about relative dates in your local context.
