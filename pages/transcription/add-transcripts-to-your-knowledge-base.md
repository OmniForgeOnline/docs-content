A transcript is not useful to the Assistant until it is indexed into the workspace knowledge base.

## Add a transcript

1. Open **Transcription**.
2. Select a completed session.
3. Review the title and transcript.
4. Choose **Teach AI**.

![OmniForge transcript detail view with transcript editing and Teach AI action.](/images/screenshots/transcript-detail.webp)

The summary is indexed immediately and appears with other knowledge sources.

## Ask about the recording

Try:

> What were the main decisions from yesterday’s meeting?

Or:

> Who is responsible for the follow-up tasks we discussed?

## Keep workspace context in mind

Recordings belong to a specific workspace. If a session is in the wrong workspace, select it and use the Move action to transfer it to the correct workspace before teaching the AI.
