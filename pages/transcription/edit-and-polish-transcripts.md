Transcript cleanup helps the Assistant retrieve better evidence. You do not need to rewrite everything, but important names and decisions should be clear.

## Review the session

1. Open **Transcription**.
2. Select a completed session.
3. Read the beginning, key decisions, and any sections with names or dates.
4. Rename the session if the title is unclear.

![OmniForge transcript detail view with transcript editing and add-to-knowledge actions.](/images/screenshots/transcript-detail.webp)

## Polish when useful

Use polishing when you want a cleaner version for reading or indexing. Keep the original meaning intact.

Good candidates for polishing:

- Meeting summaries.
- Dictated notes.
- Interviews with repeated filler words.
- Transcripts with important decisions.

## Preserve evidence

Do not edit a transcript in a way that changes what was said unless you clearly mark your own notes.
